I am an AI student at the Faculty of Computer Science, University of Information Technology–VNUHCM. Currently, I am a Research Assistant at AISIA Research Lab and a research student at The UIT Natural Language Processing Group (NLP-UIT Group) (previously a research student at ELO Lab). My research interests center around Natural Language Processing (Segmentation-Free & Phonemic Modeling), Adversarial Robustness & Evolution Strategies, and Ontology-Guided Hierarchical RAG.
